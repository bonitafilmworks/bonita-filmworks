Who Are We?

Bonita FilmWorks Company is a production group consisting of brothers Lukas David & Jacob Billups, named after our late grandmother who inspires us every day with her fearless attitude.
Writer-Director Lukas David is interested in telling stories of lived experiences from various walks of life that unite the human condition. Cinematographer Jacob Billups brings his passion for visual storytelling into the equation.
We are passionate about the connecting and communal element of filmmaking. It takes a village of skilled craftspeople to create a film and we are trying to curate a village of like-minded people to bring perspective to the stories we would like to share.

Bonita FilmWorks Company is available for hire to impart our sense of aesthetic to your project.
