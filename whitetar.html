whiteTAR

dir. Lukas David released-2025 runtime-6 min.

A drug-addled relationship goes from bad to worse when Lola crosses a new line, heroin.

In my first foray into narrative filmmaking, I wanted to tell a story I knew. In the past, I witnessed drugs cause many relationships to form and deteriorate. I wanted to share my perspective on the way aspiration and expectations can cause toxicity in a relationship.
