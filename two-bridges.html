Two Bridges, New York

dir. Lukas David premiered-2026 runtime-9 min.

An immigrant fisherman aims to make a better life for him and his delinquent son with a lucky talisman pulled from the East River. Unfortunately, he is unaware the Chinese Triad is also after the talisman.

Having spent my High School years in Chinatown, I've always had a fascination with the fishermen and wanted to tell a story centering them. I used this character, Siu, to tell a story asking how much luck does it take to get a piece of the American Dream.
